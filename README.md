# Chat Kyi for iPhone (beta)

Chat Kyi and his alter ego Thamein Paw Taw (သမိန်ပေါတော) on your home screen, with voice, mic, both personas, the teaching shelf, and recording mode.

## 1. Put the app online (5 minutes, free)
Your iPhone needs a web address to open it from.
1. On your Mac, unzip this folder.
2. Go to https://app.netlify.com/drop and sign in.
3. Drag the whole **chat-kyi-iphone** folder onto the page.
4. Netlify gives you a link like `https://something-random.netlify.app`. Text it to yourself.

The link only contains the app, not your key. Anyone who opens it would need their own API key.

## 2. Install on your iPhone
1. Open the link in **Safari**.
2. Tap Share, then **Add to Home Screen**, then Add.
3. Open Chat Kyi from your home screen and paste your Anthropic API key (from console.anthropic.com). It's stored only on your iPhone.

## 3. Make sure you can hear him
- **Turn off Silent mode** (the switch or Action button) and turn the volume up. iPhone mutes this voice in Silent mode.
- iPhone only allows speech after a tap, so tap the screen once after opening the app.
- Allow the microphone and speech recognition when asked. If you said no before: Settings, Apps, Safari, Microphone, Allow.

## Talking to them
- Tap the big **mic**, talk, and stop. He answers out loud.
- Say a name to switch persona: "Chat Kyi, ..." or "Thamein Paw Taw, ...". You can also tap the name at the top.
- **Keep talking** makes them listen again after every reply, so you don't have to tap each time.
- Typing works too.

## Voices and accents
Settings, then Voices: pick Chat Kyi or Thamein Paw Taw and set each one's accent, voice, depth and speed.
To get more accents: iPhone Settings, Accessibility, Spoken Content, Voices. Download Chinese, Vietnamese or other English voices (Singapore, India, UK, Australia), then reopen the app.

## Teach them
Teach, then paste article text. In Safari, the Reader view (aA icon) makes it easy to select all and copy an article. Both personas reread the shelf before every reply.

## Recording
- Pick Green screen in Settings, then **Hide controls**, then use iPhone screen recording. Double-tap the top of the screen to bring the controls back.
- The Log keeps every reply in English and Burmese with Say again and Copy buttons.

Good to know: the chat log, settings and shelf are saved on this iPhone only. Deleting the home screen app erases them.
